General Questions

There’s an array of diverse breeds of buffaloes all over India. These vary in size, type, color, weight, feeding habits, milk production capacity, lifespan, and other factors. You’ll find different breeds in a specific region.

The Murrah breed is considered one of the best for dairy farming due to its high milk yield and adaptability to various climates.

The price of a Murrah buffalo varies based on factors like age, breeding history, and milk production potential. On average, prices can range from INR 40,000 to INR 80,000 or more.

Other Questions

The factors that determine the cost of a Murrah buffalo include:

  1. Body features (size, structure, etc.)
  2. Milk yielding capacity
  3. Lactation cycle
  4. Hereditary history

A Murrah buffalo can typically be milked for around 250 to 300 days in a lactation cycle before it enters a dry period.

The dry period for a Murrah buffalo generally lasts for about 60 days before it is ready to be milked again after giving birth.

There are many factors involved in choosing the right species of livestock for your farm. While cows produce more milk than buffaloes do, buffalo milk is high in fat content, making it ideal for dairy products like ghee and paneer.

India is home to a wide variety of buffalo breeds including Murrah, Nili-Ravi, Jaffarabadi, and Surti, each having unique features that suit different farming needs.

Murrah buffaloes are highly recommended for dairy farming due to their high milk yield and good adaptability in various conditions.

The Murrah breed can produce an average of 10–15 liters of milk per day, depending on the quality of care and nutrition provided.

The cost of a Murrah buffalo ranges from INR 40,000 to INR 80,000 or more, depending on various factors like health, age, and milk yield potential.
